# Load Ledger

A phone-first training log for lifting, running, metcons and other cardio. It turns your sessions into a weekly report of load per body part, cardio output and effort.

- **Log**: start a lifting, run, metcon or other cardio session.
  - **Run**: distance, time, average HR, calories and type (easy, long, tempo, intervals, race, recovery). Pace shows per km and per mile.
  - **Metcon**: name, format (for time, AMRAP, EMOM, intervals, chipper), workout description, time or time cap, score, Rx or scaled, HR and calories. Optional movements with total reps and weight count their load toward the body-part report (load only, not hard sets).
  - **Other cardio**: bike, row, SkiErg, swim, walk, elliptical, stairs, with distance, time, calories and HR. Pace shows per 500 m for rowing and SkiErg, per 100 m for swimming and as km/h for the bike.
  - **Distances**: enter them in km, mi or m. Everything converts to km automatically, and reports always use km.
- **Log from a photo**: take or choose a photo of a whiteboard, notebook page, or a watch or app screenshot. Claude reads it and builds the sessions (lifting, runs, metcons, cardio), converting lb and miles to your units. You review and untick anything wrong before it's added. Exercises it doesn't recognise are added as your own exercises, with the muscles they work.
- **After each session**: rate the whole session's RPE (1–10), add the duration and write notes. RPE × minutes gives a session load figure.
- **Lifting**: start or repeat a workout, add exercises, and enter weight × reps. Last time's numbers show as hints, and "Use these" copies them in. Tap **W** to mark a warm-up set so it doesn't count.
- **Report**: pick a week to see workouts, hard sets and total load compared with the week before. It includes:
  - a Cardio output section with total cardio time, distance (km, and miles alongside), calories and average HR, plus a breakdown by type (run, metcon, row, bike…) and a 6-week chart of minutes
  - an Effort & notes section with average RPE, session load and each session's rating and notes
  - a front/back body heatmap, shaded by hard sets or by load
  - notes when running distance rises more than 10%, cardio time jumps 30%, session load jumps 30% or average RPE runs very high, plus muscles over 20 sets, untrained muscles and when to deload
  - a per-muscle table grouped by body part, with 6-week sparklines
  - "Copy as text" for sharing the report
- **Journal**: a daily note with sleep, energy (1–5), soreness (1–5) and free text, a chart of your recent session RPEs, and a day-by-day timeline of notes and sessions.
- **History**: past workouts grouped by week. Tap one to edit it.
- **Settings**: kg/lb, whether you usually enter runs in km or mi, the day your week starts, your bodyweight (counted on pull-ups, dips and similar lifts), and your own exercises with main and helper muscles.

How load is counted: an exercise's main muscles get a full set and its helper muscles get half a set (bench press = 1 chest, ½ triceps, ½ front delts). Load = weight × reps, credited the same way.
